Wars

bring everyone

You want a war? We accept. Bring your crew, bring your best, bring everything you've got.

Open a ticket in #war-requests with your crew name, roster size, and preferred time. We'll match it — or raise it. Team Z, Y, X, or A will answer.

Request in Discord

Match Rules

  • No third-party scripts. Clean fights only.
  • Roster locked 30 minutes before start.
  • Best of 3, or agreed-upon format.
  • Team captains confirm the lineup in voice.
  • Winner takes bragging rights. Loser takes notes.

Banned

  • No control
  • No transformation
  • No portal V and C
  • No run
  • No scripts
  • No externals (matcha, matrix, etc.)
  • No pain
  • No dragon heart
  • No dragon storm
  • No V4
  • No heal on NPC

These are not the final war rules. War fighters may update or add rules live in the war GC before a match.
