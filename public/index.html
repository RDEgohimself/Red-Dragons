Wars

bring everyone

You want a war? We accept. Bring your crew, bring your best, bring everything you've got.

Hit the button and it drops you straight into the war-requests channel. Post your crew name, roster size, and preferred time. We'll match it — or raise it.

Request in Discord

Match Rules

  • No third-party scripts. Clean fights only.
  • First to 5, unless changed by war fighters.
  • Region may change, or cross-region may be agreed.

Banned

  • No control
  • No transformation
  • No portal V and C
  • No run
  • No scripts
  • No externals (matcha, matrix, etc.)
  • No pain
  • No dragon heart
  • No dragon storm
  • No V4
  • No heal on NPC

These are not the final war rules. War fighters may update or add rules live in the war GC before a match.
